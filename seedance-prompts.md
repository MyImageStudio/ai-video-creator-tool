## 💡 Pro Tips for Seedance 2.0

1. **Start with shot type** — wide/medium/close-up always first
2. **Add camera movement** — push in, pull back, pan, tilt, drone
3. **Specify lighting** — neon, golden hour, backlit, dramatic, soft
4. **Add "cinematic"** — dramatically improves quality
5. **End with quality tag** — "8K", "photorealistic", "ultra detailed"
6. **Avoid negatives** — describe what you WANT, not what you don't want

## ❌ Common Mistakes

| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| "A person walking" | "Medium shot, person walking slowly through rain" |
| "Beautiful sunset" | "Wide shot, dramatic sunset over ocean, golden hour, cinematic" |
| "No blur" | Just describe sharp: "crisp focus, sharp details" |
